## 1. Stage 3: Three Creative Direction Explorations

### Concept 1: "Beep to Receipt" (Selected)
One physical barcode scan at an emart24 checkout register touches hardware, operating system, network, application, cloud automation, and human processes. The entire portfolio is structured as the vertical strata of that single transaction journey, descending from the laser optics at the till down through kernel device drivers, Layer 1-4 routing, POS transaction engines, and cloud reconciliation. The journey culminates at the printed thermal receipt, which serves as Aries' verified professional experience, credentials, and contact dossier. An unbroken SVG trace line links the strata, encoding scroll progress and grounding abstract computing in a tangible physical event.

### Concept 2: "The Hardware Disassembly Workbench"
Treating the portfolio as an exploded-view technical manual of a retail workstation. Visitors zoom through an interactive schematic view of physical motherboard chips, serial ports, RJ45 interfaces, and storage disks. Selecting any physical component opens technical field notes explaining real diagnostic incidents (e.g. thermal printer driver conflicts, broken copper pairs on pins 4/5, power supply failures). While tactile and grounded in Aries' hardware technician background, it over-indexes on physical mechanics at the expense of cloud, SaaS, and software development foundations.

### Concept 3: "The Interactive Incident Dispatch Log"
Structuring the entire portfolio as a real-time engineering incident ledger. Each section represents a timestamped ticket transition: Detection, Triage, Isolation, Vendor Escalation, Root-Cause Postmortem, and Preventive Automation. While highly relevant to operational support, it risks recreating the corporate helpdesk aesthetic that the owner explicitly asked to escape, and fails to give adequate space for pure computing curiosity and creative laboratory experiments.

## 2. Stage 4: Decision & Selection Justification

**Adopted Concept:** **"Beep to Receipt"**

**Justification:**  
"Beep to Receipt" is uniquely suited to Aries Razak's authentic background. As a System Application Specialist at emart24 who supports nationwide retail store POS, payment PIN-pads, network switches, and cloud sync, a retail checkout scan is his daily reality. Rather than relying on generic sci-fi tropes or corporate dashboard clichés, it turns an ordinary physical action into an extraordinary educational journey through computing layers. It perfectly satisfies every non-negotiable rule:
1. It tells the truth without exaggerating titles.
2. It organizes verified experiences into logical physical strata (Hardware $\rightarrow$ OS $\rightarrow$ Network $\rightarrow$ Application $\rightarrow$ Cloud $\rightarrow$ People/Process).
3. The thermal receipt provides a natural, accessible, ATS-friendly summary that satisfies recruiter needs within one click.
4. It accommodates interactive simulations (Packet Flight, Cache Eviction, UNIX Bitmask) seamlessly within their native strata.
